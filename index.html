OpiArto — Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 August 2026

OpiArto collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no way to. The app has no internet permission and contains no networking code, so everything you type into it stays on your phone.

The rest of this page says the same thing in the detail a reviewer, or a careful reader, will want.

Who is responsible

OpiArto is published by Aurora CAM Solutions. Questions about this policy, or about the app, go to auroracamsolutions@gmail.com.

What the app holds, and where

OpiArto is a study planner. It keeps what you put into it:

  • Assignments, projects, exams and their deadlines, notes and tasks
  • Courses, timetables and class times
  • Attendance you have marked, and grades you have entered
  • Time you have tracked against a piece of work
  • Your settings, including the name you choose to be greeted by

All of it is written to the app's own private storage on your device, which no other app can read. None of it is sent anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to be sent: the app requests no INTERNET permission, and Android will not let an app without it open a network connection.

Permissions, and why each one exists

PermissionWhat it is for
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To show you a reminder before a deadline, and to show a running study timer in the notification shade. The app asks for it on its own notification settings screen rather than at first launch, so you can see what it would be used for before deciding. Refusing it leaves the rest of the app working.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Android clears every scheduled alarm when the phone restarts. This lets the app lay your reminders down again afterwards, so a phone restarted on Sunday is not a week without reminders.

There are no others.

Files you choose yourself

Two features touch files, and both happen through Android's own file picker — the app never browses your storage on its own, and receives only the single file you point it at:

  • Importing a timetable. You pick a calendar export (.ics) that your school gave you. It is read on the device, turned into classes and deadlines, and that is the end of it.
  • Backup and restore. You can write everything the app holds to a single JSON file, wherever you choose to put it — your own storage, or a cloud folder if that is where you send it. That file is yours: the app does not upload it, and cannot. If you place it in a service's folder, that service's privacy policy applies to it, not this one.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling OpiArto deletes everything it holds, because everything it holds is on your device. There is no server copy to ask about and no account to close. If you have written backup files, deleting those is up to you.

Your rights under the GDPR

The GDPR gives you the right to see the personal data an organisation holds about you, to correct it, and to have it erased. Aurora CAM Solutions holds none: nothing you enter into OpiArto ever reaches us, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete. You hold your own data, on your own device, and the app gives you a way to export all of it and a way to destroy all of it.

Children

OpiArto is a study planner, and it may well be used by people under 16. That changes nothing here: the app collects no personal data from anyone, of any age, and transmits none.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever gains a feature that touches the network — there is no such plan — this page will say so before that version is released, and the date at the top will change. The page is public and its history is public, so what it said on any given day can be checked.
